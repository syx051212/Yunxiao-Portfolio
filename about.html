About Me.关于我

From International Journalism to Media Arts and Design
从国际新闻到媒体艺术与设计

My name is Yunxiao Song, and I am from Beijing, China. Before coming to the United States, I studied International Journalism for two years. I am currently studying Media Arts and Design at the University of Missouri–Kansas City.

我叫宋昀晓，来自中国北京。在来到美国之前，我学习了两年国际新闻。现在，我正在密苏里大学堪萨斯城分校学习媒体艺术与设计。

My experience in journalism taught me how to observe people, conduct interviews, research topics, and tell stories through writing. Through different journalism projects, I developed a strong interest in people, culture, and visual storytelling.

新闻学的学习经历让我学会了如何观察人物、进行采访、研究主题，以及通过文字讲述故事。通过不同的新闻实践，我逐渐对人物、文化和视觉叙事产生了浓厚的兴趣。

After studying journalism, I wanted to explore more creative ways of communicating ideas. I began expanding my work from written stories to photography, video production, directing, editing, poster design, and other forms of visual media.

学习新闻学之后，我希望探索更多富有创造力的信息表达方式。因此，我开始将自己的创作从文字故事扩展到摄影、视频制作、导演、剪辑、海报设计以及其他视觉媒体形式。

For me, moving from International Journalism to Media Arts and Design is not leaving storytelling behind. Instead, it is a way to discover more possibilities for telling stories through both words and images.

对我来说，从国际新闻转向媒体艺术与设计并不意味着离开叙事，而是通过文字与影像探索更多讲述故事的可能性。

In the future, I hope to become a writer, screenwriter, director, or content creator who combines writing with visual storytelling.

未来，我希望成为一名能够将文字与视觉叙事结合起来的作家、编剧、导演或内容创作者。
