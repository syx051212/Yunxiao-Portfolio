Portfolio.个人作品

My portfolio includes video production, journalism and writing, poster design, and promotional material design. These projects show my creative journey from International Journalism to Media Arts and Design.

我的作品集包括视频制作、新闻与写作、海报设计和宣传物料设计。这些作品呈现了我从国际新闻走向媒体艺术与设计的创作历程。

01 / Video Projects｜视频作品

01

Knots in Bloom绳上花开

About the Project｜作品介绍

This documentary interview tells the story of Zhang Yanrong, an inheritor and teacher of Beijing’s intangible cultural heritage rope art. It explores how she combines traditional knotting techniques with modern design and uses her craft to support young mothers, retired residents, and the wider community.

这支人物采访纪录短片讲述了北京非遗绳艺传承人与教师张艳荣的故事，展现她如何将传统编绳技艺与现代设计相结合，并通过绳艺帮助年轻妈妈、退休居民和更多社区成员。

My Role｜我的职责

Independent producer — story planning, interview-outline writing, interviewing, filming, editing, and writing the final feature article.

独立负责全部制作流程：故事策划、采访提纲撰写、人物采访、现场拍摄、后期剪辑及最终人物新闻稿写作。

02

International Beijing国际化的北京

About the Project｜作品介绍

Created to promote Beijing as an international city, this bilingual video uses the letters in “BEIJING” as a creative structure to introduce different aspects of the city. Through interviews with international residents and scenes of their daily lives, the video presents Beijing’s culture, diversity, openness, and global connections.

这支中英双语视频以宣传北京的国际化形象为目的，创意性地拆分“BEIJING”中的每个字母，以此串联并介绍城市的不同方面。视频通过采访在北京生活的国际友人，并记录他们的日常生活，呈现北京的城市文化、多元特点、开放氛围和国际联系。

My Role｜我的职责

Filming and video editing — selecting footage, organizing the visual narrative, and shaping the overall rhythm of the final video.

主要负责现场拍摄与后期剪辑：筛选素材、组织视觉叙事，以及把握成片的整体节奏。

04

Mozi’s Rules — A Bilingual Physics Micro-Lesson墨规万物——中英物理微课

About the Project｜作品介绍

Created for the National College Computer Design Competition, this bilingual micro-lesson connects Mozi’s scientific ideas with modern optics, acoustics, and mechanics. 这部中英双语微课为全国大学生计算机设计大赛创作，将墨子的科学思想与现代光学、声学和力学联系起来。通过视觉衔接和信息组织，将抽象的物理知识转化为直观易懂、富有吸引力的教学故事。

My Role｜我的职责

Beijing municipal-level award｜北京市市级奖项

05

Rewind倒带

About the Project｜作品介绍

Rewind was my first complete short film, created while I was studying in Beijing for a creative camp organized by my university’s television station. Inspired by real disagreements within our team, the story rewinds a failed collaboration and gives the characters another chance to listen to one another and work together.

《倒带》是我在北京读书期间，为学校电视台创意营创作的第一部完整短片。故事灵感来自团队成员之间真实发生的意见分歧：一次失败而混乱的合作被“倒带”，让成员重新获得倾听彼此、共同完成创作的机会。

My Role｜我的职责

Director and screenwriter — scriptwriting, creative development, leading the filming, and contributing to the editing. I guided the project from its initial concept to the final presentation.

导演与编剧：主要负责剧本撰写、创意构思和现场拍摄，同时参与部分后期剪辑，并推动作品从最初构想到最终成片的完整呈现。

First place, university television station creative camp｜学校电视台创意营第一名

03 / Poster & Visual Design｜海报与视觉设计

Poster and Social Media Design｜海报与社交媒体视觉设计

01

Reading Culture Festival Promotional Posters读书文化节宣传海报

About the Project｜作品介绍

This poster series was created for the 19th Reading Culture Festival at Beijing International Studies University. It includes an opening-ceremony poster and a program schedule introducing the event’s activities, time, and location. Inspired by the theme “Begonias in Bloom, Books Bring Fragrance to BISU,” I combined books, flowers, and soft spring colors to create a fresh and literary visual atmosphere.

这组海报为北京第二外国语学院第十九届读书文化节设计，包括开幕式宣传海报和活动节目单，主要介绍活动内容、时间与地点。围绕“海棠花开，书香二外”的主题，我将书本、花朵与清新的春日色彩相结合，营造出自然、温暖且富有书香气息的视觉氛围。

My Role｜我的职责

Independent Designer — Concept Development, Layout Design, Typography, Image Selection, and Information Organization

独立设计——创意构思、版式设计、字体编排、图像选择与信息整理

02

Student Organization Recruitment Posters学生组织招新海报

About the Project｜作品介绍

This recruitment poster series was created for the Youth League Committee of the School of Culture and Communication. It uses a lively Memphis-inspired style, contrasting black, purple, and white colors with cartoon illustrations to communicate a youthful and energetic atmosphere. Clear information sections introduce the recruitment time, location, member benefits, and the responsibilities of different departments.

这组招新海报为文化与传播学院团委设计。作品采用活泼的孟菲斯视觉风格，以黑色、紫色和白色的对比配色搭配卡通人物，展现年轻、有活力的组织氛围。海报通过清晰的信息分区，介绍招新时间、地点、加入组织的收获以及不同部门的主要职责。

My Role｜我的职责

Independent Designer — Visual Concept, Copywriting, Information Hierarchy, Illustration Arrangement, and Layout Design

独立设计——视觉构思、文案撰写、信息层级规划、插画编排与版式设计

03

WeChat Article Cover Designs微信公众号文章封面设计

About the Project｜作品介绍

These WeChat article covers were designed for campus cultural events, including an invitation to “A Spring Gathering at Dongguan” and an announcement of the “Lanting Cup” calligraphy competition results. Drawing inspiration from classical Chinese painting and calligraphy, I used ink-style illustrations, traditional colors, and balanced typography to communicate the cultural themes of each article.

这组微信公众号文章封面为校园文化活动设计，包括“春禊有约·东观雅集”传统文化活动邀请函，以及“兰亭杯”书法大赛获奖结果推文封面。我从中国传统书画艺术中获取灵感，运用水墨插画、传统色彩和协调的文字排版，呈现不同文章的文化主题。

My Role｜我的职责

Independent Designer — Visual Concept, Image Selection, Typography, Composition, and Final Production
